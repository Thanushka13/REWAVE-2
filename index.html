⚠ WATER SAFETY NOTICE

Basic filtration alone cannot guarantee microbiological safety. Recovered water must receive treatment and testing appropriate to its intended use. Unsuitable water must be diverted rather than reused. Non-potable plumbing must remain separated from drinking-water systems. This website is a student concept demonstration, not a certified treatment system.
